## 主流音乐 App 主页

### 模式

- **Recents shortcut grid (2-column compact tiles)**：A dense 2-column grid of short horizontal tiles (art flush left, 1–2 line bold title) at the very top of home, under the chips. 6–8 items, and odd counts are fine. Small extras: an inline play glyph on play-first items and a blue dot for new or unplayed content.（见于：Spotify home (c2609ecf, b0868f3e, d1a1d2ce)）
  - 对 Yoin：High. It is close to Yoin's Jump Back In grid already. The unread dot (for example a playlist changed on the server or an artist with a newly added album) and the inline play glyph are low-cost extras. In edit mode it is a natural candidate for pinning or removing individual tiles.
- **Top filter chips that re-scope the feed**：A chip row pinned under the header that swaps the whole feed (All/Music/Podcasts, or moods like Workout/Energize/Feel good). The selected chip is filled, and seasonal chips appear temporarily (Wrapped).（见于：Spotify (c2609ecf, d1a1d2ce, 898b4d92)、YouTube Music (a84ec650, 6bd60afe, 908f5102)、Amazon Music (ff23447d)）
  - 对 Yoin：Medium. Yoin has no podcasts, and Subsonic genres are messy free text. Chips scoped to genre, decade or 'unplayed/forgotten' could work, but they compete with the customizable home. A more likely use is one temporary chip, such as a monthly recap.
- **Top-picks hero carousel with a reason eyebrow**：Large portrait or landscape cards (60–90% width, neighbors peeking), each labeled with a short reason ('Mood for You', 'NEW ALBUM FOR YOU', 'Featuring X'). The reason sits either above the card (Apple) or as a colored eyebrow in the footer (Tidal). The play button overlaps the art.（见于：Apple Music Top Picks (9290ae13)、TIDAL hero (9f4bc387)、Apple Music New tab (5c79bb05)）
  - 对 Yoin：High, provided the reasons are local and honest: 'Rated ★★★★★ · not played in 6 months', 'Added this week', 'Turns 10 today', 'Has your notes'. All of these come from Room (ratings, play_history, notes) plus Subsonic album metadata. As a full-width section it should be movable but probably not resizable.
- **Personal infinite station ('Flow' / 'My Soundtrack')**：One play-first item that starts an endless personalized stream. It appears as a gradient wordmark tile with a big play circle (Deezer), a full-bleed photo hero with seed artists listed and a FAB (Amazon), or a 'create a radio' builder card (YouTube Music). Tuning comes from a mood or genre dial and a discovery-versus-favorites toggle (Deezer). The FAB morphs to equalizer bars while playing.（见于：Deezer (82d59a7d, bd86b415, 9d0a3cc3, 3e4ae39f)、Amazon Music (ff23447d, 8479afa0)、YouTube Music music tuner (4cab88d2)）
  - 对 Yoin：High. It is the strongest single addition for a library player: a 'Yoin Flow' over your own library. Prefer the familiar↔forgotten axis over moods, since Subsonic has no mood tags, and genres could feed a dial. In the grid it works as a 1×1 or 2×1 play-first cell, or a full-width hero, and the FAB-to-equalizer morph fits Expressive motion.
- **Quick picks / Start listening paged track columns**：A track list of 3–4 rows (cover, title, artist, overflow) that pages horizontally, with the next column peeking. The header has an eyebrow ('START RADIO FROM A SONG') and a 'Play all' pill. Tapping a row starts radio or a queue from that track.（见于：YouTube Music Quick picks (a84ec650, 6bd60afe, 908f5102)、Spotify Start listening (b0868f3e, a00b0de3, 898b4d92)、Apple Music Latest Songs (3d34a415)、Amazon Music Trending Songs (f36cd455)）
  - 对 Yoin：High. It brings track-level entry points to a home that is album-heavy today. Paging keeps the section height fixed (good for a stable grid in edit mode). Song notes or a rating star can sit inline on rows.
- **Ranked chart list (rank numerals, plays, trend arrows)**：Numbered rows with rank numerals. Variants add inline play counts ('14 Plays'), green/red trend arrows under the numeral, a play-circle button on each row, or giant numerals behind covers. There is usually a 'See the full list >' footer.（见于：Apple Music Replay Top Songs (ec5c8c61)、YouTube Music Top songs (a390cf65)、Pandora Top Thumb Hundred (ff8ea117)、Amazon Music (f36cd455)、Brink giant numerals (6d31e5b3)）
  - 对 Yoin：High. Everything is local, so it works for every provider profile that records history. 'Your top this month' with trend arrows, or 'Highest rated' (the analogue of Pandora's thumbs), turns Yoin's ratings into a home component. Giant numerals behind covers are a good Expressive treatment.
- **Listening recap / milestones**：A recap entry point as a typographic poster card on a gradient (Apple Replay), a hero banner with CTA (Spotify Wrapped) or a plain text row (Pandora Playback). It comes with milestone badges that carry a date ('500 Artists Played · Reached 19 June').（见于：Apple Music Replay (90ea4184, ec5c8c61)、Spotify Wrapped (d1a1d2ce)、Pandora (943c8668)）
  - 对 Yoin：High, and fully local. A monthly recap card tinted from the top album's palette and milestone badges (1,000th play, 100 albums rated, first note) are distinctive for a self-hosted player that has no cloud 'Wrapped'. Suitable as a 1×2 or 2×2 cell that rotates content.
- **Mix tiles with generated collage covers**：Square mix or station tiles whose cover is generated: a 2×2 collage of artist photos (TIDAL), a wordmark band over a two-photo split (Deezer DAILY), a solid color with overlapping circular faces and a 'RADIO' tag (Spotify), or a colored name strip across a photo. Captions read 'Featuring A, B, C…' or 'A, B and more'.（见于：Deezer Made for you (82d59a7d)、TIDAL Mixes For You (3a59548f)、Spotify Recommended Stations / top mixes (df111c93, c2609ecf, 8baed828)）
  - 对 Yoin：Medium-high. Local mixes by genre, decade or rating band are cheap. The important part is the cover system: compose from album art plus the palette color, never a blank tile. It fits Yoin's 1×1 grid cell, and a 2×2 block of four mixes mirrors Deezer.
- **Seeded 'Because you…' shelf with avatar header**：A shelf whose header names its seed: a circular avatar plus an eyebrow ('BECAUSE YOU ADDED', 'For fans of', 'More like', 'Discover more from') and a bold entity name, sometimes with a trailing chevron.（见于：TIDAL (f5701dff)、Spotify (8baed828, 1925c9b8)）
  - 对 Yoin：Medium. The pattern works best with honest library-based seeds: 'Because you starred <album>' → more from that artist or genre, 'Because you rated <artist> 5★'. Avoid implying recommendations Yoin cannot make. It should be a full-width shelf section.
- **Favourite artists circle row**：A horizontal row of large circular artist photos (about 3 visible) with centered names. Circles mean artist and squares mean album or playlist, consistently.（见于：Deezer (bd86b415)、YouTube Music Your favorites (4cab88d2)、Spotify Popular artists (1925c9b8)）
  - 对 Yoin：High. Most-played artists from local history. Yoin's per-entity MaterialShapes (artist = pinwheel or another Expressive shape) can replace plain circles. Images can be missing on Subsonic, so a fallback is needed.
- **New-from-followed story circles with unread badge**：Small avatar circles with a colored badge at the corner, story-ring style, showing 'new from' a followed creator.（见于：SoundCloud New tracks (2ec02900)）
  - 对 Yoin：Medium. It is a nice 'new on your server' signal for people who keep adding music. Unread state needs a small Room table, and it pairs with the unread-dot idea on grid tiles.
- **Resume / continue card**：A wide card for the single most likely next action: art on the left, eyebrow plus title plus description on the right, and save plus a large play button. Widgets use the same unit at two sizes (small = art + play; wide = art + play + strip of recents).（见于：Spotify next episode awaits (b0868f3e)、Spotify widgets (d7a177a7)）
  - 对 Yoin：High, especially when nothing is playing (it complements the bar idle state). A 2×1 'pick up where you left off' cell with a position ring. The widget size pair is a good model for how one Yoin cell renders at 1×1 versus 2×1 when resized in edit mode.
- **Recently played / recently added shelf with type and quality labels**：A standard horizontal shelf of square covers. Mixed-type shelves label each item inside the card ('Track Radio', 'Artist Radio') with a centered play overlay. Album shelves show the year plus quality badges ('MASTER', 'ULTRA HD', 'ATMOS').（见于：TIDAL (874250d6, 7a4af934, f5701dff)、Apple Music (b936aa8f)、Amazon Music (f36cd455)、SoundCloud (2ec02900)）
  - 对 Yoin：High. Yoin already has Recently Added and Activities. Adding 'FLAC 24/96'-style badges is something a self-hosted audiophile library can actually show, and type labels clarify the mixed Activities bento.
- **Genre / mood / decade tiles**：A grid of category entry tiles: dark tiles with a thin colored accent stripe (YouTube Music, 2 columns × 3 rows, horizontally paged), solid colored blocks (Deezer, Pandora) or icon tiles. Often closed by a plain text-link list ('More to Explore').（见于：YouTube Music Moods & genres (a9626e3b)、Pandora Browse By Category (943c8668)、Apple Music More to Explore (8c9a4984)）
  - 对 Yoin：Medium. Useful as a compact launcher into Library filters. Accent-stripe text tiles need no artwork, so they scale to messy genre lists. This is better as one optional section than a top-level feature.
- **Expanded preview card (cover + 3 tracks + actions)**：A large carousel card showing the playlist cover, title and count, a one-line description, the first three tracks as tappable rows, and a row of circular actions (play / radio / save).（见于：YouTube Music Recommended playlists (81864bca)）
  - 对 Yoin：High for user playlists and a 'featured album'. It removes a detail-page round trip, and the actions map to Play / Shuffle / Star. As a full-width section it needs fixed height for edit-mode stability.
- **Time-of-day / weekday contextual section**：Copy that names the moment ('Soundtrack your Saturday evening', 'Your afternoon lift', 'At night'), sometimes drawn as a vertical timeline with nodes. Greetings are folded into a playable header ('Welcome <name>' + Play all).（见于：Spotify (898b4d92)、Headspace (005702a0)、YouTube Music (6bd60afe)）
  - 对 Yoin：High, and fully local: 'Your usual weekday mornings' built from history. Low implementation cost and high perceived intelligence. A timeline line could echo the note-anchor visual language.
- **Now-playing context inside home**：Home items reflect playback: the current track row turns accent-colored with animated equalizer bars, and a hero's play FAB morphs to bars. The mini player is tinted from the art.（见于：Spotify (a00b0de3)、Amazon Music (8479afa0)、Deezer mini player (82d59a7d)）
  - 对 Yoin：High. It fits the motion-first identity (spring icon and shape morph). It must use a narrow projection so per-tick position does not recompose home (respect the NP position dedup invariant).
- **Dismissible tip / onboarding card**：A soft rounded card at the top of home with an icon, one sentence, an X to dismiss and a primary button.（见于：Apple Music (9290ae13)）
  - 对 Yoin：Medium. It is the cleanest place to teach the new long-press edit mode once ('Long-press to rearrange your home'), or to prompt setup such as a missing Spotify Client ID, then never show again.
- **Offline / cold-start honest empty states**：Inline sections that explain absence instead of erroring: 'While you're offline — your downloads will appear here', or a centered 'Welcome! Start listening for more personalized picks' on a fresh account.（见于：Spotify offline (ac1946e9)、Pandora cold start (943c8668)）
  - 对 Yoin：High. A self-hosted server can be unreachable, and new profiles start with empty history. Keep cached sections, replace network-only ones with a quiet row, and fall back to library-only sections (Recently Added, Random) on cold start.
- **Reroll / refresh for randomized sections**：A full-width outlined 'Refresh Recommendations' button under a set of randomized cards.（见于：Calm (4a107313)）
  - 对 Yoin：Medium-high. Cheap with Subsonic, and a good spring moment (cards spring out and new ones in). Could also be a small header action instead of a full button.
- **Friend / server activity**：Social posts in the form 'X reposted a track · 1h' with art, comment and engagement counts.（见于：SoundCloud Feed (f8984c3f)）
  - 对 Yoin：Low-medium. A full social feed is out of scope (YAGNI). For multi-user family servers, a tiny 'Also playing on this server' strip from getNowPlaying is the only realistic version, and only on Subsonic profiles (capability-gated).
- **Edit-state affordances (remove badge + drag grip)**：Rows in edit state show a red circular '−' at the leading edge and a three-line grip at the trailing edge, so editability is visible without relying on motion alone.（见于：SoundCloud Next Up (e2fb791f)）
  - 对 Yoin：High, as a reference for the long-press edit mode. Pair the jiggle with a corner '−' badge (hide section) and a visible grip or lift shadow on sections and cells. In edit mode, full-width sections reorder vertically, Jump Back In cells reorder within the grid, and 1×1 ↔ 1×2 resizing can follow the widget size pair (d7a177a7).
- **Content-tinted header / immersive top**：The top of home picks up color from the first content: a blurred gradient behind header and chips (YouTube Music), or a full-bleed artist photo under the status bar (Amazon).（见于：YouTube Music (908f5102, 6bd60afe)、Amazon Music (ff23447d)）
  - 对 Yoin：High, and already aligned with Yoin's palette and aurora wash. Color changes must animate and be deduplicated by value (theme wash invariant).

## 听歌统计 / 日记类与小组件式仪表盘

### 模式

- **Period-scoped KPI row (minutes / artists / tracks)**：Three to four small stat tiles for a fixed window (this week, this year, all time), often under a hero 'time listened' number, sometimes phrased as a sentence ('You listened for 952 minutes in July').（见于：Airbuds Widget、Brink、Apple Music Replay、Spotify Listening stats）
  - 对 Yoin：High. It is cheap to compute and works as a 2×1 or 2×2 card. Pair it with a period chip (Week / Month / Year) that springs between values.
- **Top-N with big total + bars**：A large total headline ('21 total airports') followed by a ranked bar list with counts and a scope toggle (All-time / Year).（见于：Flighty、Brink (Top Podcasts)、YouTube Music Charts (rank movement)）
  - 对 Yoin：High for 'Top artists', 'Top albums' and 'Top genres'. Add up/down/new movement markers by comparing this week's ranks with last week's.
- **Entity-shaped 'top' tiles**：Each 'top' stat gets a form matching its entity: circle for artist, square for song, vinyl for album, scalloped badge for genre.（见于：Spotify Listening stats、Shelf profile、Apple Music Replay (rank numerals over images)）
  - 对 Yoin：Very high. It maps directly to Yoin's entity-type→MaterialShapes backdrop rule and gives 1×1 tiles identity without extra chrome.
- **Size-as-rank podium**：Covers scaled by play count and bottom-aligned, so rank reads as size instead of as a list.（见于：TIDE (Most Used Focus)、Apple Music Replay (large rank tiles)）
  - 对 Yoin：High and motion-friendly: sizes animate with spatial springs when the period changes. Good as a 2×1 'Most played this month' card.
- **Listening-days heatmap / streak**：A GitHub-style contribution grid or month calendar tinted by activity, optionally showing current and best streak. Duolingo joins consecutive days into a single capsule.（见于：Brink、timespent、Atoms、TIDE、Duolingo、Noom、Liven）
  - 对 Yoin：High, but keep it gentle and non-gamified (no freezes or punishments). The merged-capsule streak is a nice spring morph, and the heatmap scales from 1×1 to 2×2.
- **Cover calendar**：A month grid where days with activity show a cover thumbnail instead of the date number.（见于：Hypelist）
  - 对 Yoin：Very high for a library owner. Each day shows that day's most-played album cover, which is visually rich and opens a full diary.
- **Listening diary log**：Month-grouped rows with a boxed day numeral, cover, title, half-star rating and small glyphs for liked, repeat and note.（见于：Letterboxd、Plex、IMDb (personal vs community rating)）
  - 对 Yoin：High. It surfaces Yoin's own data (ratings, notes, starred). As a home card, show the last 3 entries and link to the full diary.
- **On this day / N years ago**：A date header with 'N years ago' and tilted or scattered covers or photos, paged by year, sometimes with a note snippet.（见于：Placify、Canopi、Abode Memories）
  - 对 Yoin：High. It complements the existing Memories surface. Show it only when there is a match, and degrade to 'a month ago'.
- **Multi-size widget variants**：One data source rendered as small, medium and large cards with different information density (the small card often uses the cover-tinted background).（见于：Shazam、timespent、Jomo、Apple Fitness、Yazio）
  - 对 Yoin：Essential for a resizable home grid. Each HomeSection or card type declares supported spans (1×1, 2×1, 2×2, 1×2) and a layout per span.
- **In-place edit mode (badges + grips + add slot)**：The real home stays visible and switches to an edit state: minus badges on removable cards, grip dots on movable tiles, dashed empty slots or an 'Add card' tile, Cancel/Save or Done, and 'Reset to default'.（见于：Bevel、Binance、Garmin Connect、Shelf、timespent）
  - 对 Yoin：Best match for the long-press-to-jiggle request and Yoin's inline edit-mode decision. Long-press enters edit, cards wobble subtly, badges scale in with an effect spring, and the grid lines fade in.
- **Drag feedback: lift + ghost + neighbour displacement**：The picked card lifts (shadow, slight scale), a faded ghost stays at the origin, and neighbours slide to open the target slot.（见于：Revolut、Future Pro、Shelf、GitHub (Edit My Work)）
  - 对 Yoin：Required for a convincing jiggle mode. Use spatial springs for offset and scale, an effect spring for ghost alpha, and haptics on pickup, slot-change and drop.
- **Constraints and safety nets in edit mode**：Some sections can be reordered but not removed, capacity limits are stated ('up to 8'), sizes that don't fit are disabled, a reset-layout link is offered, and copy reassures that hiding doesn't delete data.（见于：Sonos、Garmin Connect、Binance、timespent (LARGE dimmed)、Gentler Streak、Ubank (eye toggle)）
  - 对 Yoin：High. A fixed 3×4 JBI grid needs disabled-size feedback, Activities could be pinned (reorderable only), and a hide toggle is gentler than delete.
- **Discoverable non-gesture entry to edit**：An 'Edit Home' or 'Edit' pill at the end of the home feed, plus a list-based editor for precise reordering.（见于：Sonos、Bevel、Ubank Money App、Dropbox）
  - 对 Yoin：Recommended as a fallback and accessibility path alongside long-press; the list editor is also easier with TalkBack.
- **Insufficient-data / pending states**：A card that explains when it will populate ('Keep listening to unlock more stats', 'first weekly recap will drop on Sunday'), or empty drop slots with copy.（见于：Spotify、Airbuds Widget、Shelf ('Drop item here')）
  - 对 Yoin：Important because each profile is an independent data source and Spotify/Apple Music profiles start with little local history.

## 自定义 / 编辑模式流程

### 模式

- **Dual entry: discoverable button + long-press shortcut**：Most apps enter edit mode with an explicit control: an 'Edit Home' or 'Customise' pill at the end of the feed (Sonos, Bevel, Craft, Ubank, Monzo), an 'Edit' link on a section header (Apple Health, Apple Photos, MyFitnessPal) or an overflow menu (Apple Music, Apple Podcasts). Long-press works as a power-user shortcut: Binance's long-press widget menu has an 'Edit Homepage' item, and Copilot and timespent rely on long-press and teach it with copy or a placeholder tile.（见于：Sonos、Bevel、Craft、Ubank、Monzo、Apple Health、Apple Photos、MyFitnessPal、Apple Music、Apple Podcasts、Binance、timespent、Copilot Money）
  - 对 Yoin：Strong fit. Long-press any Activities, JBI or Recently Added tile, with a haptic: the tile lifts and the whole home enters edit mode with that tile already picked up, so moving the finger drags it straight away. Also add a tonal 'Edit home' pill after the last section so people can find edit mode without the gesture. This matches the 'inline edit-mode chosen' decision for customizable home Phase 2.
- **In-place edit state with corner badges (the jiggle analogue)**：Cards stay where they are and keep their content, but each removable card gets a small circular minus badge on its top-start corner, partly outside the card edge (Apple Fitness, Bevel, Garmin, Binance, the Arc illustration). Some tiles also get a grip at the top-end corner (Garmin, Shelf, Bevel tiles). Required items show their status differently: dimmed with no badge (Apple Fitness Activity Ring), a lock (Deel) or a greyed checkbox (Origin). MyFitnessPal blurs content and overlays an action pill.（见于：Apple Fitness、Bevel、Garmin Connect、Binance、Arc Search、Shelf、Deel、Origin、MyFitnessPal）
  - 对 Yoin：Strong fit for the bento and the JBI 3×4 grid. No app shows iOS-style wobble. Android has no jiggle convention, so the 'alive' cue should be small: a subtle phase-offset rotation or a breathing scale driven by a spring, and none when animator duration scale is 0. Pair it with a slight scale-down (~0.96) of all tiles and the corner badges popping in with a spatial spring. Ordinary cover tiles must stay border-free (cover-borders rule), so the edit cue should come from badges, scale and a dashed outline on empty cells only.
- **Lift + reflow drag feedback**：The lifted item gets a white or raised surface, a drop shadow and a slight size increase, sometimes breaking out past the list inset to full width (Asana, Copilot, Apple Music, Apple Health). Neighbours open a landing gap before the drop (Apple Podcasts). Revolut leaves a faded ghost in the origin slot while the card floats over the live feed.（见于：Revolut、Asana、Copilot Money、Apple Health、Apple Music、Apple Podcasts）
  - 对 Yoin：Strong fit. Lift with a spring on scale (~1.04) and on shadow or tonal elevation. Neighbours reflow with spatial springs (LazyGrid animateItem). Snap to JBI cells, where a 1×2 card claims two cells and the cells it displaces cascade. Give a haptic tick each time the drop target changes, and a confirm haptic on drop.
- **Two-bucket add/remove (active + catalog)**：Editors list current items with minus badges or trash icons, and below them a catalog of available items with plus badges or pin icons (Apple Photos 'Suggestions', Evernote 'Available widgets', Binance 'Recommend', Apple Health categories with search, Garmin 'other stats'). In-grid adding uses dashed placeholders ('+ Add card' in Bevel, '+ Add Goal Card' in MyFitnessPal, '+ Add' cells in Shelf, empty slots in Binance) or a '+' button in the header (Revolut, Apple Fitness, Deel '+ Add widget'). Capacity limits are stated up front ('up to 7', 'up to 8').（见于：Apple Photos、Evernote、Binance、Apple Health、Garmin Connect、Bevel、MyFitnessPal、Shelf、Revolut、Apple Fitness、Deel）
  - 对 Yoin：Good fit. Removed sections or tiles go to an 'Available' tray rather than being deleted. In edit mode, empty JBI cells become dashed '+' cells that open a picker (recent albums, playlists, a song note, a rated album). The 3×4 grid sets the capacity, so a full grid can grey out '+' as timespent does.
- **Size variants (S/M/L) with fit constraints**：Size is chosen from a small fixed set: timespent's SMALL/MEDIUM/LARGE stamps (LARGE greys out when it can't fit), Binance's square and wide icons at the top of the long-press menu, Tide Guide's Small/Medium/Large/XL segmented control, and Box Box Club's S/M/L chips. Oura uses pinch to resize. Widget galleries (Tiimo, Abode) show that each size has its own content-density rules.（见于：timespent、Binance、Tide Guide、Box Box Club、Oura、Tiimo、Abode）
  - 对 Yoin：Good fit, and it goes beyond the current fixed 1×1 plus 1×2. Offer 1×1, 1×2 and 2×2 through an M3 Expressive connected button group in the long-press menu or on the selected tile in edit mode. Disable sizes that don't fit. Morph the tile's shape and size with spatial springs. Define what each tile kind shows at each size (cover only, cover plus title, cover plus note text).
- **Long-press context menu as the quick-edit gateway**：The pressed tile lifts sharp over a blurred or dimmed page, with a menu attached to it. Binance's menu has inline size icons, 'Choose Coin' (swap content), 'Edit Homepage' (enter full edit mode) and 'Remove Widget'. Wabi's has 'Add to widget' / Share / Remix / Delete.（见于：Binance、Wabi、Monzo）
  - 对 Yoin：Strong fit, and it settles the hold-versus-release question. Long-press then release opens a YoinDropdownMenu-based menu (all menus must use this wrapper) with Size / Change… / Edit home / Remove from home. Long-press then move starts a drag and enters edit mode. Blur the backdrop with the effect-spring alpha.
- **Section-level list editor (sheet or in place)**：For reordering or hiding whole sections, apps switch to a compact list: rows with a check, toggle or eye plus a grip (Asana, Dropbox, Craft, Sonos, Ubank, Apple Music, Apple Podcasts, Origin, Copilot). Craft updates the home behind the sheet live. Apple Music and Apple Podcasts turn the page itself into the list.（见于：Asana、Dropbox、Craft、Sonos、Ubank、Apple Music、Apple Podcasts、Origin、Copilot Money）
  - 对 Yoin：Medium fit. Dragging tall sections like Recently Added in place is awkward. In edit mode Yoin could collapse each section to a compact header 'pill' (shape morph) that can be reordered and hidden in place, then expand back on Done, which avoids a separate sheet. A sheet with a live preview behind it (Craft) is the fallback.
- **Exit and commit semantics**：Two models appear. Auto-save with a single Done: a check circle (Apple Fitness lime, Apple Health blue, Apple Music), 'Done Editing' (MyFitnessPal), X and check in a bottom bar (Shelf, timespent), or just dismissing the sheet (Craft). Transactional editors use Cancel/Save (Bevel, Garmin, SiriusXM, Dropbox Save, Origin CANCEL/APPLY), often followed by a confirmation toast ('Your changes have been saved', 'Widget has been deleted', 'Pinned to Home').（见于：Apple Fitness、Apple Health、Apple Music、MyFitnessPal、Shelf、timespent、Craft、Bevel、Garmin Connect、SiriusXM、Dropbox、Origin、Deel、Sonos）
  - 对 Yoin：Prefer the launcher-style model: auto-save with Done, where the floating bottom bar morphs into an edit bar (Add / Reset / Done), plus Undo for destructive steps. Flag for the parent: Home is a RootSection, and AGENTS.md forbids intercepting root back for local UI, so 'back exits edit mode' needs an explicit design decision. The options are Done or tap-outside only, or treating edit mode as a shell overlay that shares the back controller.
- **Undo, reset and safety nets**：Bevel puts 'Reset to default' at the bottom of edit mode, and Binance has a reset slot inside the grid. Deel shows a delete snackbar. Character AI leaves an in-place blurred tombstone ('Post hidden' + Undo). Medium and others use an Undo banner or snackbar.（见于：Bevel、Binance、Deel、Character AI、Medium）
  - 对 Yoin：Strong fit. 'Remove from home' outside edit mode shows a tombstone in the slot, or a snackbar with Undo, before the grid reflows. 'Reset to default' sits in the edit bar's overflow and needs confirmation.
- **Pin to Home from content pages**：Items reach home from outside the editor: Sonos 'Pin Collection to Home' with a toast that navigates home, Bluesky 'Pin to home' flipping to 'Unpin', Apple Health 'Pin to Summary' / 'Unpin from Summary' with an explanatory caption, Apple Photos Pinned with Suggestions, and YouTube Music showing a pin glyph on pinned library items.（见于：Sonos、Bluesky Social、Apple Health、Apple Photos、Perplexity、YouTube Music）
  - 对 Yoin：Good fit. Add 'Pin to Home' on album, playlist and artist detail pages and in long-press menus. It creates a JBI tile, followed by a toast with a 'View' action that scrolls home to the new tile, which pops in with a spring. Pinned tiles are user-owned and deletable, while built-in sections can only be reordered (Sonos rule).
- **Teaching the gesture in context**：timespent fills empty grid slots with illustrated 'LONG PRESS TO EDIT' tiles. SiriusXM shows a dismissible tip 'Press and drag an item to move it.' Copilot's copy names long-press directly. Oura shows a one-line hint pill 'Drag to move. Pinch to resize.' Arc Search shows a tutorial sheet of the launcher edit mode.（见于：timespent、SiriusXM、Copilot Money、Oura、Arc Search）
  - 对 Yoin：Good fit. When JBI has empty cells, show one onboarding tile ('Long-press to arrange') that disappears after the first edit. Show a one-time hint pill the first time edit mode opens ('Drag to move · tap − to remove').

## 交互暗示与可发现性

### 模式

- **Lift-in-place + anchored menu (card long-press)**：On long-press, the pressed card stays at its own position but lifts (sharper, brighter or enlarged, sometimes reshaped into a wider preview card) while the rest of the page blurs or dims. A compact menu anchors directly beside it. Menus often start with an icon row of 2–3 primary actions, then grouped lists with destructive items in red at the end. Dismiss = tap outside.（见于：Apple Music、Binance、Wabi、WhatsApp、Edits、Savee）
  - 对 Yoin：Strong fit for long-press on a JBI tile or Activities card. Drive the lift with a spatial spring (scale/elevation) and the scrim with an effect spring, and fire a haptic at the threshold. Route the menu through the existing YoinDropdownMenu wrapper, never raw M3 DropdownMenu. Put play/next/favorite/rate as the top icon row.
- **Item menu as the doorway to edit mode**：The card's own long-press menu includes a mode switch ('Edit Homepage', 'Select multiple', 'Select') next to per-item actions (remove, resize, change content). This resolves the ambiguity of a gesture that means both 'act on this item' and 'rearrange the page'.（见于：Binance、WhatsApp、SmartThings）
  - 对 Yoin：Recommended answer to 'what does long-press on a CARD mean vs on empty feed space'. Card long-press = item menu whose last group is 'Edit Home' (plus 'Hide' and 'Resize' where the card supports it). Long-press on empty feed space or a section gutter = enter edit mode directly, mirroring the OS home-screen convention.
- **In-place edit (jiggle) mode on the feed itself**：The home feed transforms in place: remove (minus) badges on section or tile corners, drag handles, Cancel/Save or Done in the top bar, a dashed '+ Add card' slot at the end, and 'Reset to default'. A grid stays a grid rather than collapsing into list rows.（见于：Bevel、Garmin Connect、Finch (illustrating iOS)、timespent）
  - 对 Yoin：Matches Yoin's chosen inline edit-mode direction for the Phase 2 home editor. Badges and handles should spring in with a slight stagger, and tiles can do a subtle continuous wobble as the 'you're in edit mode' signal. Keep it subtle and loop it only while visible, like the aurora loops. Exiting edit mode should be a reversible, back-aware state, not a new route.
- **Direct drag-to-reorder with clear landing feedback**：The picked-up item floats (scaled up, shadowed, sometimes semi-transparent) while either a ghost stays in the origin slot (Revolut) or a dashed placeholder marks the landing slot (Deel). Siblings shift to make room. Some apps allow this straight from long-press without a separate edit screen.（见于：Revolut、Deel、Klarna、Linear Mobile、Linktree、The Weather Channel、Apple Health）
  - 对 Yoin：For the JBI 3x4 grid, show a dashed or tinted target cell under the finger and let displaced tiles move with spatial springs, with a haptic tick on each cell crossing. 1x2 cards (song note, rated album) need span-aware landing rules: show the 2-cell target, or refuse with a rubber-band if it doesn't fit.
- **Persistent, low-intrusion edit entry points**：Always-present but quiet affordances: an 'Edit Home' button at the very end of the feed, an 'Edit' text link on a section header, a '+' on a section header, and a 'Reset' that is disabled until changes exist.（见于：Bevel、Apple Health、Revolut、Klarna）
  - 对 Yoin：Yoin should ship at least one of these alongside the hidden long-press, because long-press alone has poor discoverability. Best fit: an 'Edit Home' tonal button after Recently Added (users reach it after scrolling everything), and optionally a header-level edit affordance per section.
- **Self-describing placeholder tiles (the hint is the content)**：An empty or unconfigured slot carries its own instruction ('Tap and hold to set up', a mascot holding 'LONG PRESS TO EDIT', dashed '+ Add …' cards with an x). The hint disappears when the slot is filled or dismissed.（见于：Notion、timespent、Satispay、Finch）
  - 对 Yoin：Very good fit for new profiles or sparse libraries. Instead of blank JBI cells or an empty 1x2 note/rated card, render a quiet tile such as 'Press and hold to rearrange' or 'Rate an album to fill this'. It needs zero overlays and teaches at the exact spot.
- **Motion or pulse hints**：A pulsing or glowing target ('TAP & HOLD'), a touch-ripple illustration, or an edge peek of the next card to imply a gesture. Ambient rather than blocking.（见于：Deepstash、Finch、Quizlet）
  - 对 Yoin：Best match for the user's 'long-press wiggle' idea: after N home visits without edit mode ever being used, one JBI tile plays a single small spring wobble (or a brief lift-and-settle) on idle. Cap it at 1–2 times, and never repeat it after the first successful long-press. This is inferred design, not something observed animating on Mobbin; the screens are static.
- **Coach marks and spotlight tours**：Dimmed page with a bubble or spotlight pointing at a control, a step counter (1/3, 7/7), and Next/Got it/X. Usually first run, sequential and modal.（见于：MD Vinyl、Acorns、Grab、monday.com、Opera、Tabby）
  - 对 Yoin：Use sparingly. At most a single non-modal spotlight over the JBI grid the first time Home opens after the editor ships. The Acorns variant (spotlight leaves the control interactive) is the least annoying because the user can try the gesture immediately.
- **Gesture legend overlay**：A full-screen blurred overlay listing gestures with hand pictograms, dismissed by tapping anywhere. Shown on first entry to a gesture-heavy surface.（见于：Telegram、Polarsteps、World App、Uber Eats、Reddit）
  - 对 Yoin：Too heavy for Home. Possibly appropriate for Now Playing or for the first time edit mode opens (drag to move, tap minus to hide, pinch or handle to resize).
- **Transient confirmation HUD after a quick action**：A small centered pill toast (icon + 'Playing Next', 'Favorited', 'Added to Library') appears above the mini-player after a context-menu action and fades out.（见于：Apple Music）
  - 对 Yoin：Use this for long-press quick actions on home cards (Play next, Favorite, Hide). It should originate near the card or bar and use an effect spring for alpha, consistent with the bar and pill motion family.
- **Pin-to-home and feed-tuning actions in item menus**：Content menus include 'Pin Album/Playlist', 'Pin Collection to Home', 'Hide', 'Suggest More/Less' or 'Exclude from taste profile', so users shape Home from the content itself instead of only from an editor.（见于：Apple Music、Sonos、Apple Books、Apple News、Spotify）
  - 对 Yoin：Add 'Pin to Jump Back In' and 'Hide from Home' to album/playlist menus. This gives Home customization without visiting edit mode and keeps everything local and per-profile (no provider write needed).
- **Multi-select via long-press (lists, not feeds)**：In libraries, long-press (or Select) enters multi-select: checkboxes slide in, a count pill or header appears, and a floating action bar offers batch actions. On image grids, selected items get a backing plate instead of an overlay.（见于：Shazam、Crouton、Apple News、Matter、Todoist、Apple TV）
  - 对 Yoin：Keep long-press-to-multi-select for Library and detail tracklists. On Home, long-press should not start multi-select; that would collide with the edit-mode and item-menu meanings.
