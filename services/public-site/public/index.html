Yoin

An Android music client by GojyuPlusOne.

Connect your music, browse your library, and keep ratings, notes and album memories.

Music services

  • Subsonic, OpenSubsonic and Navidrome: connect your own server and account.
  • Spotify: connect your account. Playback requires the Spotify app, Spotify Premium and a configured Spotify Client ID.
  • Apple Music: connect your account. Subscription playback requires an active Apple Music subscription.

Yoin does not provide a music catalog or a subscription to these services.

Optional services

Use your own Gemini API key for song information, lyric translation and generated Memory text. You can also choose to synchronize selected Yoin data through your Google Drive account.
