## Top 10 (Claude's pick from the high-severity rows)

1. **A Mac-started Spotify queue can stop after the current song** (B › Spotify, "Automatic queue advancement"): the end-detection path ends playback instead of starting the next queued song.
2. **Search is shallow**: only Spotify's first 10 results per type, no further pages; no playlists in results; no library-vs-catalog scope; no type filter (A › Search).
3. **Now Playing rating only takes 2/4/6/8/10** (B › Now Playing): Android's tenths can't be entered from the NP bar.
4. **Detail pages have no back stack** (A › Detail): album → artist replaces the column; Back closes it instead of returning to the album.
5. **Library is incomplete**: no "All" view, no Favorites (starred) view, and very large libraries are capped (A › Library).
6. **Queue list stops at 80 upcoming songs** (B › Queue): the rest can't be seen, reordered or removed.
7. **Translate can silently switch the lyrics source** (C › Gemini / lyrics): Android asks before swapping in another provider's lyrics and timing.
8. **No reconnect flows**: an expired Spotify sign-in or Google (sync) authorization has no Reconnect on the account / Sync page (C › Accounts, Sync).
9. **Removing the active account drops to sign-in** even when other accounts remain (C › Accounts).
10. **Focus entered from About shows no lyrics** (D › rules audit, design.md focus rule: the current lyric always stays in focus).

Close behind: mirrored Spotify playback (no history rows, the mode button doesn't change Spotify's mode, Up Next doesn't show Spotify's queue), Gemini Ask / target-language picker / About retry missing, Tips reader absent, backup match correction and earlier versions missing.
